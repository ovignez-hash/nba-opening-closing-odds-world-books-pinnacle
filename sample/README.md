### How to read it

**Prices are decimal odds** (European format), up to 4 decimals: `1.91`, `2.375`. A stake of 1 returns the price, stake included.

**Moneyline includes overtime.** It is the two-way home/away market.

**1X2 is regular time only:** home win, draw, away win after four quarters, overtime excluded. A draw at the end of regular time is a loss for both teams' 1X2 prices.

**The spread line is the home team's handicap.** `-6.5` means the home team gives 6.5 points; the away team's line is the opposite. Spread and total include overtime.

**The total line is in points**, the same for over and under.

**One line per book, market and phase: the main line.** At the open, it is the first line the book offered: among the lines it posted in its first two hours, the most balanced one (two prices closest to each other). When that line was clearly not the main one, with its two prices more than 0.50 apart, no opening price is given (3,659 cases). At the close, it is the most balanced of the lines the book updated in its last hour of updates, provided its two prices are at most 0.15 apart; otherwise the most balanced of all its lines.

**One time per book, market and phase.** The `_Time` columns are ISO 8601 in UTC and give the moment the price was recorded. Most prices are stamped to the minute (seconds `00`); Pinnacle's are stamped to the second, as are 65% of Betway's 2026-27 time stamps and 8 of William Hill's.

**The opening price is the first price recorded for the main line.** Median age of the opening moneyline at the scheduled tip-off: Pinnacle 21 hours, Bet365 17 hours, William Hill 19 hours, Bwin 24 hours, Betway 20 hours, Betfair Exchange 23 hours, SBOBET 18 hours, 1xBet 27 hours. No opening is older than 14 days.

**Openings older than 14 days were left out** (1,628 prices, 431 games). A first price recorded more than 14 days before tip-off belongs either to the original date of a postponed game, priced for a day it was not played (in March 2020 for games played in the Orlando bubble in August, for instance), or to a line posted months ahead when the schedule came out.

**Placeholder and reversed openings were left out:** an opening moneyline with the same price on both teams while the other books opened more than 0.10 away from a toss-up in no-vig probability, or, when no other book opened, while the closing market is more than 0.15 away (38 prices); and an opening moneyline whose two prices were reversed: swapped, they match the median of the other books' openings within 0.05 (with fewer than two other openings: the closing market), while as published they are more than 0.15 away from it and from the close (42 prices).

**The closing price is the last price the book recorded before tip-off.** `Kickoff_UTC` is the scheduled tip-off; the ball is usually tipped a few minutes later and books take bets until then. Every closing price is recorded between 12 hours before and 30 minutes after the scheduled tip-off. A price is recorded when it changes, so a line the book did not move in its last hours carries an older time. Median closing time against the scheduled tip-off:

The time columns give it game by game.

**Closing prices were checked against the market.** For each game, the reference is the median of the closing values of all the other books recorded for that game, including books that are not in this file, taken as recorded, before this check: the no-vig home probability on the moneyline (each side's 1/price divided by the sum of both), the line on the spread and the total. A closing price was left out when it was more than 0.05 away in no-vig probability on the moneyline, 2.5 points or more away on the spread, or 3 points or more away on the total (2,363 prices). With fewer than three other books recorded, a spread or total line at least that far from every other recorded book's line was left out (17 prices). A spread line more than 5 points away from the line implied by the same book's moneyline (−12.5 × the inverse normal of the home no-vig probability) was left out too (30 prices at the close, 22 at the open when both opened within 3 hours of each other). Opening prices more than 0.30 away in no-vig probability, 8 points on the spread or 15 points on the total from that same reference were left out (156 prices).

**Prices that cannot exist were not used:** prices below 1.01; two-way markets with a margin below 1% or above 12% (Betfair Exchange: below 0% or above 6%); 1X2 margins outside 2-20%; spread and total prices outside 1.40-3.50; total lines outside 150-290 points, spread lines beyond 30 points and lines that are not a multiple of 0.25; opening prices recorded after the closing price or after the tip-off. The margin is checked on the main line once it is chosen: when it fails, the book has no price for that market and phase. When the opening record is the closing record itself (same moment), it appears once, as the closing price.

## Said plainly

- **Pinnacle starts on 16 October 2018** (2018-19), with the moneyline and the total; its spread starts on 30 July 2020, with the restart in the Orlando bubble. No Pinnacle price exists for 2008-09 to 2017-18.
- **Before the 2020 restart, Pinnacle's closing price is its last recorded price:** a median 5.4 hours before the scheduled tip-off in 2018-19 and 5.1 hours in 2019-20 before the pause. From the restart, it is recorded at tip-off (median 9 minutes after). For 145 games (87 of them from 3 to 13 March 2026), that record is missing or was taken after the game had started; their closing price is Pinnacle's last pre-game price from a second timestamped record (median 4 minutes before).
- **2008-09 is a partial season for the books:** Bet365 starts on 19 January 2009, Bwin and Betway on 29 January 2009, Betfair Exchange on 11 February 2009.
- **623 games have no price at all:** 602 in 2008-09, up to 19 February 2009, and 21 in 2010-11 (8 of the 13 games of 17 January 2011, and a few others in January, March and April 2011). They stay in the file so that the schedule is complete.
- **William Hill carries the moneyline only**, from 3 June 2010 (the 2010 Finals). It is absent from the start of 2025-26 to 31 March 2026, and back from 1 April 2026.
- **Betfair Exchange carries the moneyline only.** Its prices appear to be net of the exchange's 5% commission on winnings: the median margin at the close is 2.8%, above Pinnacle's 2.6%, whereas raw best back prices on both sides of a liquid market sum to about 100%. An exchange market usually opens before any money is matched, so most of its openings had no real price and were left out (16,810). It is missing in 2014-15 and almost absent in 2026-27 so far.
- **SBOBET** carries the spread and the total from 2010-11 (first price on 7 March 2011) to 2021-22, and the moneyline from 22 April 2019 to 2021-22.
- **1xBet prices the 1X2 from 28 October 2014, the spread and the total from 2 January 2015** (a handful of games in 2014-15, then most games from 2015-16) **and the moneyline from 31 October 2015.** It is almost absent in 2026-27 so far.
